Welcome to Bodrum Peninsula

Bodrum Peninsula is a beautiful region located in the southwestern part of Turkey. It is known for its stunning beaches, vibrant nightlife, and rich history. Visitors can explore ancient ruins, enjoy water sports, and experience the local culture and cuisine. Whether you're looking for relaxation or adventure, Bodrum Peninsula has something to offer for everyone.

- Ancient name: Bodrum was known as Halicarnassus in antiquity. - One of the Seven Wonders: The ancient Mausoleum at Halicarnassus was built as a monumental tomb for Mausolus, a ruler of Caria, in the 4th century BC. - Herodotus: The famous ancient Greek historian, often called the “Father of History,” was born in Halicarnassus. - Bodrum Castle: This impressive fortress was built by the Knights of St John beginning in the early 15th century. It now houses the Museum of Underwater Archaeology. These historical sites make Bodrum more than just a beach destination.
